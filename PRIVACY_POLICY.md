## Overview

Piraíba is an on-device Android network packet capture, protocol dissector, and `.pcap` analyzer. Piraíba operates entirely on your device and does not collect, sell, rent, or transmit your personal data or network traffic to the developer or any third party.

## How VpnService and Network Data Are Used

- **Local Loopback Capture (`VpnService`)**: When you explicitly start capture and grant Android VPN permission, Piraíba creates a local, on-device TUN interface (`tun0`) solely to capture and inspect IPv4 TCP, UDP, DNS, HTTP, and TLS packets generated by apps on your device.
- **Direct Forwarding (No Remote VPN Server)**: Piraíba does not route your traffic through any remote VPN server or proxy. Packet forwarding happens directly on-device.
- **Strictly Passive Local Inspection**: Captured packet headers, decoded protocol fields, and unencrypted payload previews are held only in a bounded in-memory ring buffer (up to 4,000 packets) for real-time inspection inside the app. Active diagnostic probes and background reverse DNS lookups are disabled.
- **Optional LAN Multicast Listener & Root Sandbox**: In the Sandbox tab, you may optionally enable local Wi-Fi multicast listening (mDNS, SSDP, LLMNR, WS-Discovery) or, on user-rooted devices, local `tcpdump` capture via `su`. Both remain strictly local to your device.
- **User-Initiated Exports**: `.pcap` files are written or shared via Android's share sheet or Storage Access Framework only when you explicitly choose **Save .pcap** or **Share**.

## Data Collection, Analytics, and Third-Party SDKs

- Piraíba collects **zero** personal information, device identifiers, advertising IDs, crash analytics, or usage telemetry.
- Piraíba contains **no** advertisements and **no** advertising or analytics SDKs.
- User-configured alert thresholds and your acknowledgment of the local VPN disclosure are stored locally in Android Jetpack DataStore Preferences on your device.
